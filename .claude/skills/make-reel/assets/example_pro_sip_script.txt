What happens if you invest ten thousand rupees every month for twenty years? Assuming a twelve percent yearly return, after five years you'd have about eight lakh rupees. After ten years, twenty-three lakh. After fifteen years, fifty lakh. And after twenty years, almost one crore, even though you only put in twenty-four lakh. That's the power of compounding: the last five years earn more than the first fifteen. Returns aren't guaranteed, so do your own research. Save this and start early.
